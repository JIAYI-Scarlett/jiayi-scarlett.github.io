Jiayi Kong

Ph.D. Candidate · Computer Graphics & 3D Generative AI

Nanyang Technological University, S-Lab  ·  Research Intern @ Tencent Hunyuan3D

I am a researcher in computer graphics and geometric deep learning, with a growing focus on 3D generation — built upon strong foundations in implicit representations, geometry reconstruction, and neural rendering. My work turns sparse, noisy, and incomplete inputs into high-fidelity 3D. I am advised by Prof. Ying He, with multiple works published at ACM TOG (SIGGRAPH) and ICML.
